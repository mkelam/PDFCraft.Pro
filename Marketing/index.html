Nano Influencer Outreach DO's

  • Personalize every message mentioning specific content
  • Offer free tool access for genuine testing
  • Propose 3-post packages for better rates
  • Allow creative freedom within guidelines
  • Track with unique promo codes
  • Build long-term relationships

Nano Influencer Outreach DON'Ts

  • Send mass generic pitches
  • Demand specific messaging
  • Negotiate too aggressively
  • Micromanage content creation
  • Skip performance measurement
  • Treat as one-time transactions
